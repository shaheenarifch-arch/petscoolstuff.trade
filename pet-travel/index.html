WHY FAMILIES ASK FOR HELP

One wrong date can ground your pet.

Every country sets its own rules, and the order matters: the microchip must go in before the rabies shot, some blood tests need months of waiting, and many health certificates must be issued within days of the flight and then endorsed by USDA APHIS. Miss a step and the airline can refuse to board your pet, or the destination can send it into quarantine.

How early to start.

Coming back to the US with a dog? Every dog needs a CDC Dog Import Form receipt, must be at least 6 months old and have a readable microchip. Dogs that spent time in a high-risk rabies country in the last 6 months need extra documents, so plan the return before you leave.

DIRECT ANSWERS

Pet travel questions.

What paperwork does my pet need to fly abroad from the US?

It depends on the destination country. Most need an ISO microchip, a rabies vaccination given after the chip, and an international health certificate issued by a USDA-accredited vet and endorsed by USDA APHIS. Some countries also need a rabies blood (titer) test, an import permit or parasite treatments.

How far ahead should we start?

Start as soon as you know the travel date. Many countries take a few weeks, but rabies-free countries such as Japan, Australia and New Zealand can take six months or more because of waiting periods after the blood test.

Do you issue the health certificate?

No. Only a USDA-accredited veterinarian can issue it, and USDA APHIS endorses it. We plan the steps and timeline, check the documents and coordinate with your vet, airline and endorsement office so nothing is missed.

What do dogs need to come back into the US?

Every dog entering or returning to the US needs a CDC Dog Import Form receipt, must be at least 6 months old and have a microchip that a universal scanner can read. Dogs that have been in a high-risk rabies country in the past 6 months need extra documents.

How much does pet travel help cost?

It depends on the destination, number of pets and how much you want handled. Send the request form and we reply by email with a fixed quote before any work starts.
