Giving a litter box as a gift

A self-cleaning litter box is a big present, so check three things first: the weight range on the product page (very small kittens may not be detected by the sensors), whether the cat is comfortable with new or noisy objects, and whether there is a power outlet near the litter area. Plan to introduce the box gradually, and remember it needs litter and, for some models, liners. Our self-cleaning litter box buying guide covers this in more detail.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good gift for a cat owner under $50?

Small, practical items work well: a scratching board, an odor remover for the litter area or a pet tracker for cats that go outdoors. All three are listed above with prices from the retailers.

Is a self-cleaning litter box a good gift?

It can be, if you know the cat. Check the model's weight range and whether the cat is comfortable with new, noisy objects, and make sure there is a power outlet near the litter area. Cats usually need a gradual introduction to an automatic box.

Will a self-cleaning litter box need refills?

Yes. Expect to buy litter and, depending on the model, waste-drawer liners. Check the product page for the refills your model uses before you give it.

Do the prices on this page include Black Friday or Cyber Monday discounts?

No. The prices shown were checked on the retailers' sites in October 2026 (MeoWant on October 13 and Traverseon on October 22), and we do not list sale prices or promo codes. Check the retailer's own page for the price you will pay.

How early should I order a gift for the holidays?

Order early, because delivery times vary by retailer, destination and shipping method. Check each retailer's own holiday shipping cut-off before you buy.

Can I return a pet gift if it does not suit the cat?

It depends on the retailer. Read the return policy on the retailer's own site before you order, especially for larger items such as litter boxes and carriers.
