Privacy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information PetsCoolStuff (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit petscoolstuff.trade, how it is used and the choices you have. We keep data collection to a minimum. We don’t sell products or ask you to create an account.

Information we collect

Newsletter sign-ups. Some pages (for example the blog and author pages) have an email sign-up form. If you subscribe, we collect the email address you enter and use it only to send you our guides and updates. You can ask us to remove you at any time by emailing agents@getservices.ai.

Messages you send. If you email or call us, we keep your message and contact details so we can reply.

Analytics. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and operation.

Cookies and affiliate links

PetsCoolStuff does not set its own tracking cookies. However, when you click an affiliate link and visit a retailer, that retailer or the affiliate network may set cookies so the purchase can be credited to us. This includes:

  • Awin. Partner store links, including MeoWant, are tracked through the Awin network, which may set a tracking cookie. See the Awin privacy policy.
  • Amazon. PetsCoolStuff participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. Amazon may place and read cookies or use web beacons when you click an Amazon link. See the Amazon Privacy Notice.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so will not stop the site from working. Read our affiliate disclosure to see how we earn from these links.

Third-party links

This site links to retailers and other websites we do not control. Their privacy practices are their own, so please read their policies before you share personal information with them.

How we use and share information

We use the information above to run the site, send the newsletter you asked for and reply to you. We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the site (such as hosting and email delivery), or when required by law.

Your rights, including California (CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on where you live, including California, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, email agents@getservices.ai. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
